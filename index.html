Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This page explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use our mobile applications and games.

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Madhavani Studios. We develop and publish mobile applications and games for Android devices. This Privacy Policy describes our privacy practices for applications published by Madhavani Studios on Google Play and other supported platforms.

Different applications may have different features and data practices. The information and permissions described below apply only to applications that use the relevant features. Please also review the information presented within the individual application and its Google Play listing.

2. Information We Collect and Process

The information handled by an application depends on its features and the services integrated into it.

2.1 Casual and Puzzle Games

Our casual and puzzle games may store game-related information on your device, such as:

  • Game scores and progress.
  • Unlocked items, skins, or virtual rewards.
  • Game preferences and settings.
  • Other information needed for normal gameplay.

Where these features use local storage, information may be stored using technologies such as SQLite or Android SharedPreferences. Our games do not require account registration unless a particular application clearly states otherwise.

2.2 Utility and Productivity Applications

Some utility and productivity applications, such as expense management tools, may process information that you provide or authorize the application to access. Depending on the application's features, this may include expense records, transaction details, bank SMS alerts, selected contacts, payment-related notifications, and reports or backup files that you create.

Where a feature is designed for local processing, the relevant information is processed on your device. Please check the individual application's disclosures for its specific practices.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Some of our free applications may display advertisements through third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob. Depending on the application and its advertising configuration, these services may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID where available.
  • Device information and technical identifiers.
  • Advertising interactions and usage information.
  • Diagnostics and performance information.
  • Information used to deliver, personalize, or measure advertisements, where permitted.

Google and its advertising partners process information under their own privacy policies and applicable requirements.

3.1 Advertising Formats

Depending on the application, advertisements may include banner, interstitial, rewarded, rewarded interstitial, app open, or native advertisements.

3.2 Rewarded Advertisements

Some applications may offer optional rewarded advertisements. These advertisements are shown when you choose to interact with a rewarded-ad feature.

3.3 Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) or another appropriate consent mechanism to collect and manage advertising consent. Where supported, users can review or change available advertising privacy choices through the application's privacy settings.

For more information about how Google uses information from apps that use its services, visit Google's Partner Sites policy.

4. App Permissions and Their Uses

Some applications request device permissions to provide specific features. Permissions vary by application. Examples may include:

4.1 Internet

android.permission.INTERNET may be used for advertisements, online services, or other network-related functionality.

4.2 Vibration

android.permission.VIBRATE may be used to provide vibration or haptic feedback during gameplay or other supported interactions.

4.3 SMS

android.permission.RECEIVE_SMS may be used by an application with an automatic transaction-detection feature to process incoming bank transaction alerts. The application's own disclosures should explain whether and how SMS information is processed, stored, or shared.

4.4 Contacts

android.permission.READ_CONTACTS may allow users to select contacts for features such as bill splitting or expense management. Contact access is used for the feature for which permission is requested.

4.5 Notifications

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS may be used to send reminders or other notifications. Where an application offers payment-notification features, notification listener access may be separately requested and enabled by the user through Android settings.

4.6 Biometric Authentication

android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC may be used by supported applications to help secure access through Android's biometric authentication system. The application does not receive the user's raw fingerprint or face biometric data through the standard biometric prompt.

4.7 Storage and File Access

Some applications may request file or storage access to create reports, export documents, or manage backups. The access requested depends on the Android version and the application's implementation. Broad storage access, including MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, is used only where applicable and permitted by Google Play policies.

5. Data Storage and Security

We aim to protect information handled by our applications using appropriate technical and organizational measures. Some applications store information locally on your device, while others may use third-party services to provide features such as advertising or online functionality.

Information stored locally may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the application's data, or remove it using the application's available features. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Sharing

Madhavani Studios does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed or shared with third-party service providers when necessary to provide application functionality, advertising, analytics, or other services integrated into an application.

For example, Google AdMob may process information for advertising purposes as described in Google's policies. The practices applicable to each application depend on the services and software components it uses.

7. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws. Where an application is directed to children or may be used by children, we take applicable children's privacy requirements and Google Play Families policies into consideration. Advertising services and other third-party integrations must also be configured appropriately for the application's intended audience.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately through one of our applications, please contact us so we can review the matter.

8. Data Deletion and User Choices

The available method for deleting information depends on the application and how that information is stored.

8.1 Games

For game information stored locally, you can generally clear the application's data through Android Settings or uninstall the application. This may remove locally stored scores, progress, preferences, and other game information.

8.2 Utility and Expense Applications

Where supported, you may delete transactions or other saved information through the application's settings or data-management features. You can also delete exported reports and backup files from your device.

8.3 Third-Party Services

Information processed by third-party services, such as advertising providers, may be subject to those providers' own privacy policies and available data controls.

9. Third-Party Links and Services

Our applications may include links to third-party websites or integrate third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites and services. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of those providers.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our applications, services, legal requirements, or privacy practices. Updates will be published on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date. We encourage users to review this page periodically.
